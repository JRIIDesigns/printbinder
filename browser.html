Downloads of 3D files, zips and pictures go into the unsorted folder of your library. Nothing is opened or run.

Find models online

Pick a site. Sign in there if you want to, find a model, and press its download button. The file lands in the unsorted folder of your library, and Review suggests where it belongs.

Your saved sites

  • Any site works: type its address in the bar at the top. ☆ Save site keeps the page you are on here for next time.
  • These pages are kept apart from the rest of PrintBinder: they cannot see your library, your files or your AI keys.
  • Sign-ins made here are remembered for this window only. Clear sign-ins forgets them.
  • "Sign in with Google" works here; some sites show it in a small window of its own. If a site will not sign you in, use Open in my browser.
  • Only 3D files, zips, pictures and instructions are downloaded here. Programs and anything else are refused.
